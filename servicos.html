Maintenance and inspection with the ship still in the water

No drydocking, no vessel downtime and no missed window. The equipment and the method are below, with the specification of every machine.

What we do below the waterline

On a ship, everything is done with the vessel in the water, with no drydocking. The same team also handles port structure work.

  • 01Hull inspection with photo and video record
  • 02General hull cleaning with a two-brush hydraulic machine
  • 03Sea chest cleaning, internal and external
  • 04Thruster cleaning, internal and external
  • 05Propeller polishing with a hydraulic machine
  • 06Sacrificial anode replacement and impressed current system support
  • 07Plugging and unplugging
  • 08Visual inspection with electrochemical potential measurement
  • 09Fiber jacket installation for underwater pipeline protection
  • 10Dredging, removal and placement of floodgates
  • 11Foundation opening for pier construction
  • 12Plate thickness measurement
  • 13Class inspection and wear-down readings

How the result is verified

Once the cleaning is finished, a representative from the ship may come aboard our support vessel and check the result live on our CCTV. The report with photographs is sent 3 to 4 business days after the service is completed.
