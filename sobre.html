The company is new. The people are not.

This is the page that answers the quiet question every shipowner asks. The answer is not the age of the company, it is the mileage of the people who go in the water: a team that already did this work together and decided to run their own operation.

The team 10+ years in the water Commercial diving · Salvador

Blue Ocean Diving was opened by a team that already worked together, in commercial diving, for over a decade. They left the company where they were and set up their own operation. We do not hide that the company is recent: we put the experience of the people who go in the water in front of it.

Along the way this team has delivered port operations on the Brazilian coast, refinery support, subsea pipeline protection with sacrificial anodes and fiber jacket, and foundation work for pier construction. Surface supplied diving, own equipment, and surface communication on every operation.
